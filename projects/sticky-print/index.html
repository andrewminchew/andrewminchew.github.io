Welcome

Hello welcome to Sticky Print! This is just a little project for printing photos and text onto standard 3” square sticky notes. If you need some guidance, click the question mark at the top of the page.

Instructions

Before you start: printing onto sticky notes can jam your printer. Laser printers heat the paper to fuse toner, and that heat can melt the adhesive and damage the printer, so inkjet printers are generally safer. Check your printer’s manual, try the whole process with a plain sheet of paper first, and use this tool at your own risk.

  1. Figure out what direction paper feeds from your print tray through your paper. You may have to look up instructions for this separately.
  2. Print the template using the “print template” button on the main page.
  3. Design your notes. There’s room for 6 sticky notes on one page. You can upload, zoom, and reposition your photos, or input any text you want, adjust the alignment, font size, and font. On photo and text notes, you can tap the bars around the outside to decide which edge should be sticky.
  4. Review your layout. On a computer, the sheet on the page shows exactly what will print. On a phone or tablet, tap the “Review and print” button to preview the sheet. Either way, make sure everything looks like you thought it would.
  5. Prep your page. Put sticky notes on the template you printed. Make sure to attach the adhesive of the sticky note onto the “adhesive here” text. If you put the notes going the wrong direction they could get stuck in your printer!
  6. Load your template into your print tray or manual feed. You’ll want to be sure that the sticky notes are being “pulled” through the printer, not pushed.
  7. Print your notes and stick em up!

Good to know

  • Your photos and text stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded; your work is saved only in this browser, so clearing your browser data will erase it.
  • To save a layout, save it as a PDF. On a computer, choose “Save as PDF” as the destination in the print dialog. On a phone or tablet, use the Open PDF or Share button and save the file to your device. A saved PDF can be printed again later, but it can’t be reopened for editing here.

Your PDF is ready

Phone browsers add a URL, date, and page number to printed web pages. A PDF prints cleanly: open it, then choose Print from the PDF viewer or share sheet.
